# FAFB v783 pilot data — provenance and use

These three compressed CSV products were downloaded by the project maintainer from the public FlyWire Codex FAFB v783 download portal on 2026-10-28 and committed unchanged for the Cortex research pilot:

- `consolidated_cell_types.csv.gz`
- `connections_princeton.csv.gz`
- `coordinates.csv.gz`

**Dataset:** FlyWire Full Adult Fly Brain (FAFB), snapshot v783 (October 2023).  
**Source portal:** https://codex.flywire.ai/  
**Download portal:** https://codex.flywire.ai/api/download?dataset=fafb  
**FlyWire citation and licensing guidance:** https://join.flywire.ai/guidelines

Before redistribution of derivative datasets or any change in use, re-check the current official terms and citation instructions.

- Dorkenwald et al. (2024), “Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain”, Nature: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y
